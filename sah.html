Acute SAH Management Protocol

Interactive Critical Care Decision Tree

1. Initial Clinical Assessment
Evaluate level of consciousness (GCS) and airway stability upon arrival.

Immediate Airway & Hemodynamic Stabilization

  • Action: Endotracheal Intubation (RSI with minimal BP rise).
  • Target SBP: Keep SBP < 160 mmHg (IV Labetalol or Nicardipine).
  • Line Placement: Invasive arterial line & central venous access.
2. Blood Pressure Management
Check current Systolic Blood Pressure (SBP).

Antihypertensive Titration

  • Goal: Titrate SBP < 160 mmHg to reduce re-bleeding risk prior to securing aneurysm.
  • Agents: Continuous Nicardipine infusion (5–15 mg/h) or Labetalol boluses.
  • Caution: Avoid sharp drops in Mean Arterial Pressure (MAP) to maintain cerebral perfusion.

4. Aneurysm Treatment Modality Selection
Evaluate anatomy and surgical risk for definitive repair:

Definitive Repair: Endovascular Coiling

  • Indication: Favorable anatomy, elderly patients, or anterior circulation aneurysms.
  • Timing: Perform within 24 hours of rupture to prevent re-bleeding.
  • Post-Procedure: Transition immediately to ICU for vasospasm monitoring.

Definitive Repair: Surgical Clipping

  • Indication: Middle Cerebral Artery (MCA) aneurysms, large parenchymal hematomas requiring evacuation, or wide-necked aneurysms.
  • Timing: Perform within 24 hours post-bleed.
  • Post-Procedure: ICU admission for ICP management and neuro-monitoring.

External Ventricular Drain (EVD) Protocol

  • Indication: Acute symptomatic hydrocephalus or GCS deterioration.
  • Action: Place EVD; level transducer to external auditory meatus (Tragus).
  • Target: Maintain ICP < 20 mmHg with controlled CSF drainage.
5. Delayed Cerebral Ischemia (DCI) Management (Days 3–14)
Standard prophylaxis for all post-repair SAH patients:

DCI Prophylaxis & Monitoring

  • Oral Nimodipine: 60 mg q4h for 21 days (essential neurovascular protection).
  • Fluid Status: Maintain strict euvolemia (avoid hypervolemia or prophylactic hemodilution).
  • Monitoring: Daily Transcranial Doppler (TCD); mean flow velocities > 120 cm/s indicate vasospasm.
  • Rescue Therapy: Induced hypertension (Norepinephrine) or endovascular intra-arterial vasodilator infusion for refractory ischemia.
