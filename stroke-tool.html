Interactive Stroke Decision Tool

Wizard: timing, infection, response to therapy, and comorbids. Button-driven — shows a single recommended outcome card.

1) Time last known well (LKW) — choose the applicable window
This determines eligibility for thrombolysis/EVT.
2) Infection status
Is there clinically suspected infection or fever? (affects sepsis workup and timing)
3) Response to initial therapy / evolution
How is the patient responding after initial interventions?
4) Comorbid / contraindication flags
Select any that apply (multiple allowed). These modify recommendations.
You can go back to change earlier answers
Result will reflect timing, infection, response, and flags.
